Life Event Triggers

Review your entire estate plan immediately if any of these events occur:

  • Marriage, divorce, or remarriage
  • Birth or adoption of a child or grandchild
  • Death or incapacity of a named executor, trustee, or guardian
  • Substantial change in estate net worth (±20%)
  • Relocation to another state or country
  • New federal tax law changes (e.g. TCJA sunset)

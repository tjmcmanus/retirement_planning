Documentation & resources

Official IRS resources

Adjustments are typically published in October to November.

  • IRS cost-of-living adjustments (tax brackets, standard deduction)
  • IRS retirement topics: contribution limits
  • IRS Publication 590-A (IRA contributions)
  • IRS Topic 409 (capital gains and losses)
  • Medicare.gov: Part B costs and Part D costs (IRMAA)
  • IRS Publication 590-B (RMDs; Uniform Lifetime Table, Appendix B)
  • SSA cost-of-living adjustments
Annual update checklist
  1. Wait for the official IRS announcements (October to November).
  2. Download the official publications.
  3. Download a copy of each table with the Download CSV button.
  4. Update each table for the new year: standard deduction, income tax brackets and capital gains brackets (both filing statuses), IRA & 401(k) limits, IRMAA thresholds.
  5. Verify every entry against the official source.
  6. Run a strategy with sample data to check the results.

The HTML app drops its cached results on every save. The Streamlit app runs in a separate process and keeps its own cache: use Refresh All Data in its sidebar (or restart it) to pick up the change there.

Common pitfalls
  • Filing status: brackets and the standard deduction have separate rows for married_filing_jointly and single. Update both.
  • Brackets: they must not overlap, and the top bracket needs a very high upper limit (for example 4000000).
  • IRMAA: it uses MAGI from two years earlier; thresholds are for married filing jointly, and single is typically half.
  • IRA limits: catch-up amounts start at age 50, with a special 401(k) catch-up at ages 60 to 63 (SECURE 2.0); Roth phase-out ranges differ by filing status.
  • Data types: years are whole numbers (2026, not "2026"); rates are decimals (0.22, not 22).

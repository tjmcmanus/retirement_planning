## Slide 7: BETR Roth Conversion Algorithm

**What is BETR?**
- Break-Even Tax Rate methodology (Vanguard research)
- Determines optimal Roth conversion amounts
- Accounts for tax payment source and time value

**Key Factors:**
- Current vs. expected future tax rates
- Years until withdrawal
- Tax payment source (taxable account vs. IRA)
- Nontaxable basis in Traditional IRA

**Decision Framework:**
- If Expected Future Rate > BETR → **Convert**
- If Expected Future Rate ≤ BETR → **Wait or reduce amount**

**Typical Results:**
- Early retirement: BETR often 15-20% (favorable for conversion)
- High earners: BETR 25-30% (requires careful analysis)

- Bunch charitable contributions
- Immediate tax deduction

**Qualified Charitable Distributions (QCD):**
- Age 70½+ from IRA

- Up to $69,000/year total (2024)
- Accelerates Roth accumulation

**Tax-Loss Harvesting:**

## Slide 14: Withdrawal Sequencing Strategy

**Optimal Order (Tax-Efficient):**

1. **Cash/Money Market** (Years 1-2)
   - No tax impact
   - Provides stability

2. **Taxable Brokerage** (Years 3-10)
   - Long-term capital gains (0-20%)
   - Tax-loss harvesting opportunities

3. **Traditional IRA** (As needed)
   - Ordinary income rates
   - Strategic conversions to Roth

4. **Roth IRA** (Last resort)
   - Tax-free growth
   - No RMDs
   - Estate planning benefit

**Key Principle:** Minimize lifetime taxes, not annual taxes
